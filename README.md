## 🧪 Featured research

### 🫁 Oncology Imaging Concept Detection

**Chest X-ray → 3D CT → lesion-centred tumour characterisation**

[![Repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/Repository-View%20Project-181717?style=flat-square\&logo=github)](https://github.com/Namitha-Narayanan-AI/oncology-imaging-concept-detection)
![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Completed-16A34A?style=flat-square)
![Domain](https://img.shields.io/badge/Domain-Oncology%20Imaging-B91C1C?style=flat-square)

My main independent research project explores the progression from **2D chest imaging to 3D lesion-centred CT modelling** using LIDC-IDRI.

The completed workflow includes:

`DICOM` · `HU conversion` · `Radiologist annotations` · `3D lesion crops` · `3D CNNs` · `Malignancy-risk modelling` · `Multi-label tumour characterisation` · `Threshold analysis` · `Clinical evaluation`

The project has shaped my broader research interests in **longitudinal oncology imaging, multimodal disease analysis, tumour-response modelling, and interpretable clinical AI**.
