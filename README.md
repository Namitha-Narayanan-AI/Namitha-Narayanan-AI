---

## Research focus

My work is increasingly centred on **medical AI for oncology**, particularly where medical imaging, quantitative analysis, and robust model evaluation can support better tumour characterisation, risk assessment, treatment-response analysis, and longitudinal disease understanding.

My MSc dissertation on medical image concept detection provided a foundation in radiology image pipelines, multi-label deep learning, and evaluation beyond accuracy. Since then, I have been developing independent research around oncology imaging, 3D CT analysis, lesion-level modelling, radiological concept learning, reproducible deep learning workflows, and clinically meaningful evaluation.

Current interests:

* Medical image analysis and deep learning for oncology
* Breast cancer imaging and imaging-based risk assessment
* 3D lesion and tumour representation
* Longitudinal and multimodal cancer imaging
* Radiomics and quantitative imaging biomarkers
* Radiogenomics and multimodal oncology AI
* Explainable and responsible clinical AI
* Robust model evaluation using clinically meaningful metrics

## Current direction

I am particularly interested in research that moves medical-imaging AI beyond isolated predictions toward understanding:

* **where disease is**
* **how individual lesions are characterised**
* **how disease changes over time**
* **how imaging and clinical information can be combined**
* **whether model outputs remain reliable across patients, datasets, and institutions**

My broader direction is toward **longitudinal oncology imaging, multimodal cancer analysis, tumour-response modelling, radiomics/radiogenomics, and clinically interpretable AI**.
